## Result
**All 38 scenarios agree within the 1e-5 tolerance** (10 models x single, five doses, 100 kg, infusion, saturating dose).
Worst normalised maximum difference 1.4e-06 (`iv_1cmt_mm:saturate`); worst Cmax 7.9e-07; worst AUC 2.1e-06.

| Case | Normalised max difference |
|---|---|
| Linear bolus models (IV 1/2/3-cmt, oral 1/2-cmt, lag, transit) | 1e-11 to 3e-10 |
| IV infusion (linear models) | 4.7e-07 to 1.2e-06 |
| Michaelis-Menten and mixed elimination | 9.8e-08 to 1.4e-06 |

The pattern is the one predicted before the run: the exact matrix-exponential path agrees with mrgsolve to ~1e-10, and the
cases PharmAgent solves numerically (infusions, nonlinear elimination; LSODA rtol 1e-7) agree to ~1e-6, which is PharmAgent's
own solver tolerance. The shared dose/observation-time convention, the lag, and the allometric weight rule produced no gap.

## What this does and does not show
- PharmAgent's simulator reproduces mrgsolve on these 38 scenarios. The independent evidence is the closed-form arm
  (`tests/test_crosscheck_mrgsolve.py`); the mrgsolve models were written by the same author from the same registry equations,
  so this arm checks the numerics, the dosing and scaling conventions, and a transcription, not the model equations themselves.
- Not covered by this PK set (see `RESULTS-PD-2026-10-16.md` for the nine PK/PD registry models):  population simulation (IIV, residual error), steady-state dosing flags,
  covariates beyond weight, anything stochastic.
